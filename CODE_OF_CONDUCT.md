# Code of Conduct / 社区行为准则

We are committed to a respectful, harassment-free community. Discuss ideas and observable behavior rather than attacking people. Feedback may be direct, but it must remain specific, professional, and useful.

我们致力于建设尊重他人、无骚扰的社区。请讨论观点和可观察行为，不攻击个人。反馈可以直接，但必须具体、专业并有助于改进。

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, threats, publishing private information, and sustained disruption. Maintainers may edit, hide, or remove contributions that violate these expectations and may restrict participation when necessary.

不可接受的行为包括骚扰、歧视、威胁、公开他人隐私以及持续干扰社区协作。维护者可以处理违反规则的内容，并在必要时限制参与。

Report conduct concerns privately through the repository owner's GitHub contact path. All reports will be reviewed fairly and with appropriate confidentiality.
