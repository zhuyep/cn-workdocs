# Delivery and QA / 交付与质检

Use this reference when the requested result is a Word document, PDF, presentation outline, or other formatted artifact.

## Before formatting

Finalize the body first. Confirm title, hierarchy, tables, source notes, unresolved markers, and required attachments before applying visual styling.

If the user supplied a template, treat it as the authority. Do not claim compliance with a named standard from visual resemblance alone.

## Word and PDF delivery

- Use paragraph and table styles consistently instead of manual formatting scattered through the file.
- Prevent table-cell paragraphs from inheriting inappropriate body indents.
- Keep headings with the following paragraph where practical and repeat table headers across pages when useful.
- Check headers, footers, page numbers, margins, list numbering, captions, and references.
- Render or export the final document and inspect every page for wrapping, clipping, blank pages, broken tables, orphan headings, font substitution, and page balance.
- Reopen the delivered file after saving to ensure it is not corrupted.

## Speech delivery

Estimate duration from the actual final word count and the speaker's likely pace. Read aloud or simulate spoken pacing. Mark emphasis sparingly after the wording is stable; excessive bolding destroys hierarchy.

## Final content audit

Verify that:

1. all `[待补]` and `[待核]` markers are intentional and visible;
2. every important number, attribution, and policy statement has a basis;
3. facts and recommendations are not blended;
4. actions identify enough of owner, object, timing, output, and acceptance criterion to be executable;
5. the title and executive judgment match the body;
6. no confidential drafting notes or hidden metadata are unintentionally included.
